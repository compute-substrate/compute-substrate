Letters

Write to anyone with a name, like guestbook. A letter is sealed on your computer and can only be opened on theirs. There's no company in the middle, so nobody else can read it, delete it or shut it down, and it's still there years from now.

Diary

Only you can ever read it. It's sealed with your key and kept by the network, so it comes back on any computer you restore your key on, years from now. To anyone else it looks like a letter, and they can't tell who it's to.

Top up

This is a web with no ads, and nobody selling your data. Reading is always free; posting, recommending and keeping sites up cost a tiny amount, which pays the people who keep the network running.

You pay by card on Stripe's page (the first time, it asks you to sign up with Link), and Doorway does the rest by itself. Stripe's page calls it USDC: that's just how the payment travels, and Doorway turns it into posts for you. Stripe's fee comes on top. Stripe will know this payment went to your key, so for writing under another name, use a separate key.
