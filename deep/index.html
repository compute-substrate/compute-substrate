Arrow keys or tap where you want to go. Walk into something to fight it, or walk away from it: a monster that has to follow you can't hit you. The rope you came down on is the way out.

    Back down
    Nobody runs the deep. It's the same twenty floors for everyone, and every ending is every move made, so anyone can play it again and see the same thing happen. The fights in each descent are drawn from a block nobody could know in advance. Whoever climbs out of the deepest floor leaves a rope there for everyone.
