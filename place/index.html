Nobody hosts it

A site doesn't run on a server, and it isn't kept by a hosting company, or by anyone you'd have to rely on. It's a set of files known by what's in them: anyone can hand it out, and Doorway checks every byte on your own computer, so nobody handing it out can change it or take it away.

Your name is yours

A name belongs to its holder's key, a year at a time. While it's yours, nobody can take it, redirect it or switch it off.

Sites that last

Anyone can keep a site up, a year at a time, for next to nothing. A site can outlive the person who made it.
