The coast

Sit down anywhere along the water, and every block that lands is a cast. Let most of it go. What you keep is yours for good: numbered, and anyone can check it.

Nobody runs this coast. What bites is decided by each new block, which nobody can know in advance, and every kept catch can be worked out again by anyone, so nobody can fake one. The coast's clock is UTC: dawn is 5 to 8.
