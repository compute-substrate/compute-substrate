    Nobody runs this coast. What bites is decided by each new block, which nobody can know in advance, and everyone's level and every kept fish can be worked out again by anyone, so nobody can fake one. The shallows are open to everyone; the reed bed from level 10, the rock ledge from 25, open water from 40. The coast's clock is UTC: dawn is 5 to 8.
